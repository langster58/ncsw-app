## 2. Car audio has changed — approved 2026-11-04

**Car audio has changed. So has the way we design systems.**

For decades, a car audio system was a shopping list. You picked a set of front speakers at one price, an amplifier at another and a subwoofer at a third. They went into the car, and you got what you got. Nothing could correct the car around them, so spending more was the only way to try for better.

Digital signal processors changed that. A processor measures how the system sounds inside the car and corrects it: the timing of each speaker, the balance between them, the peaks the car's interior adds on its own. Once that's possible, the price tag no longer decides the sound. Two things a processor can't change do: what each speaker can physically play, and how well it's installed.

That's where we put the work. We've done the math on every component we could use: how much clean output each speaker and subwoofer can produce, what each amplifier can drive, and which kinds of installs perform best in which kinds of vehicles. Every system we offer is built from those answers, with parts matched to each other and to the car, so each one does its job and none is wasted.

**The result is more sound for the money at every price, not just at the top.**

## 3. Calibration — accepted 2026-11-04

**Calibrated, not just installed.**

Once your system is in, we place a measurement microphone where you sit and set the processor so every speaker reaches you at the same moment and in balance with the others. It's the step that turns well-chosen parts into one system, and every car we build gets it.

## 7. What goes into every system — draft 2026-11-04

**What goes into every system**

- **Front speakers:** the ones you hear most, chosen for what they can play cleanly and mounted to stay that way.
- **Subwoofer:** in an enclosure built to fit your car, or built into the car itself.
- **Amplifiers:** each one sized to the speakers it drives.
- **Processor:** the piece that makes calibration possible.
- **Radio:** keep the factory radio or replace it. Either way, steering-wheel controls, calls, cameras and chimes keep working.
- **Installation:** sound treatment where the car's panels need it, and wiring built to come apart cleanly for service.
- **Calibration:** set with a measurement microphone in your car.

## 8. Common questions — draft 2026-11-04

Answers expand when tapped; the text stays in the page.

**How much does a system cost?**
It depends on your car and the system. Enter your year, make and model to see the installed price of every system we've engineered for it.

**How long does an install take?**
Most systems spend a few days in the shop. Custom work takes longer, and we'll tell you how long when we plan it.

**Is the work guaranteed?**
Yes, for as long as you own the vehicle. If something we installed stops working because of how we installed it, we fix it at no charge. Work that someone else changes afterward isn't covered. Parts carry their manufacturer's warranty, which is between you and the manufacturer. For some brands, such as Helix and Sundown, we handle the claim for you.

**Will it affect my car's warranty?**
Under US law, adding an aftermarket system doesn't void your car's warranty by itself. The manufacturer would have to show that the installation caused the problem.

**Can I keep my factory radio?**
Yes, or we can replace it. Either way, everything tied to it keeps working.

**Will my phone still work?**
Yes. Calls, voice commands and music come through the new system.

**Can I buy parts without installation?**
Yes. Call or text us with what you're looking for.
